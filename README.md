# 📊 Sales Performance & Business Insights

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance and generate meaningful business insights from historical sales data.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of sales, orders, customers, product performance, categories, customer segments, and shipping performance through interactive KPIs and visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance & Business Insights** project was created to transform sales data into an interactive analytical dashboard that helps users understand overall business performance.

The dashboard combines key performance indicators with multiple analytical views covering:

- Sales trends over time
- Product category performance
- Customer segment performance
- Product sub-category performance
- Top-performing products
- Order volume
- Customer volume
- Average order value
- Shipping performance

The report also includes interactive filters that allow users to explore the data from different business perspectives.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Track total orders
- Track the number of customers
- Analyze sales growth over time
- Compare sales across product categories
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Identify high-performing product sub-categories
- Identify the Top 5 products by sales
- Monitor average order value
- Monitor average shipping time
- Provide an interactive business reporting solution
- Convert raw sales data into actionable business insights
